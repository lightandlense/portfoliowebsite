## Trend Patterns in Brand-Native Reels Right Now

Five recurring visual patterns across luxury, athletic, denim, and jewelry brand-native feeds in 2025-2026. Russell can use these as a self-spotting checklist:

### Pattern 1: Object-on-Black (or Object-on-Void)
**What it looks like:** Single product (jewel, bag, shoe, garment) isolated against pure black or near-black backdrop. Lighting does all the work.
**Where it shows up:** Boucheron Impermanence (#14), LOEWE Puzzle Edge (#3), Cartier Trinity (#11/12), Saint Laurent show capture (#6).
**Why it works:** Strips away context, forces the eye onto craft. Maximum brand confidence signal.
**SEALCaM relevance:** This is the pipeline's sweet spot — fully synthetic, no celebrity likeness, fully controllable.

### Pattern 2: Single-Subject Garment Walk
**What it looks like:** One model, one outfit, one space, slow movement. No editing tricks, no slogan overlay.
**Where it shows up:** Prada SS26 (A2), Saint Laurent finale dresses (#6), Khaite FW25 (#8).
**Why it works:** The garment is the sentence. Confidence over story.
**SEALCaM relevance:** Highly clonable with face-agnostic models (no specific celebrity), controlled wardrobe and set.

### Pattern 3: Hand + Object Macro
**What it looks like:** Hands handling a product — often a bracelet clasp, ring slip-on, bag flap. The hand is a stand-in for the viewer.
**Where it shows up:** Cartier Trinity story (#12), LOEWE Puzzle Edge craftsmanship (#3), Tiffany Lock (#13).
**Why it works:** Tactile cue without needing a face. Inviting, intimate, no celebrity required.
**SEALCaM relevance:** Top candidate format. Hands are reliably renderable with current models.

### Pattern 4: Show-Floor Capture (Brand POV)
**What it looks like:** Brand's own camera at its own runway show — tracking shots, finale walks, ambient runway lighting. No voiceover, no slogan, just the event.
**Where it shows up:** Bottega Veneta SS26/AW26-27 (#1, #2), LOEWE SS26 (#4), Maison Margiela Artisanal 2025 (#10), Schiaparelli Metamorphosis (#9), Prada FW26 (A1).
**Why it works:** Documentary-feel implies "we don't need to sell you, we just need to show you." Highest authenticity signal in luxury.
**SEALCaM relevance:** Hardest to clone authentically — show-floor scale and crowd reaction are tells. Better as inspiration for "single-look-on-pedestal" interpretations.

### Pattern 5: Athlete-as-Sculpture
**What it looks like:** One athlete, one moment of motion or stillness, no team, no scoreboard, no logo overlay. Performance-as-portraiture.
**Where it shows up:** Nike "Best cricketers" (A3), Nike "Gully to world stage" (A4), Nike Faith Kipyegon Breaking4 (#15).
**Why it works:** Athletic version of the single-subject garment walk. The body is the sentence.
**SEALCaM relevance:** Requires specific likeness for top-tier impact (which Veo 3.1 should NOT do for real athletes); strong as a framing template for fictional/composite athletes.
