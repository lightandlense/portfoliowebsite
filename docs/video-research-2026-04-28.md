## Trend Patterns — What's Working in Brand Video Right Now (2024-2026)

### Pattern 1: Single-Object Hero Surrealism
Examples: Jacquemus bag-buses, Volvo plant-burst, oversized product placed in real environment. **Why it works:** 8-15 seconds, one visual gimmick, "is it real?" engagement loop. Cheap to produce, high re-share rate. **Russell's edge:** SEALCaM nails this in one prompt pass.

### Pattern 2: Brand-Reimagining Pastiche ("X by [luxury brand]")
Examples: Harry Potter by Balenciaga and the entire genre that followed. **Why it works:** Two unrelated cultural objects collide. Pre-built audience for both halves. **Russell's edge:** His old "100 years of fashion" / Mona Lisa transformation work IS this format. It's never gone away — the tools just got better.

### Pattern 3: Hands / Craft / Texture Close-Up Storytelling
Examples: Bottega Veneta "Craft Is Our Language," Marc Jacobs x Nara Smith "baking the tote bag." **Why it works:** Tactile counterprogramming against AI slop fatigue. Ironically, AI now renders hands well enough to clone these. **Russell's edge:** Frame composition is everything; no character continuity needed.

### Pattern 4: Static Front-Row / Editorial Capture (Couture Doll)
Examples: Doja Cat 30,000 crystals, Galliano porcelain models. **Why it works:** One subject, full ornamentation, controlled lighting. Doesn't need to move much. **Russell's edge:** Highest cloneability score in the whole doc — pure image-gen with light motion overlay.

### Pattern 5: AI Maximalism as Brand Voice (Kalshi / Genre.ai school)
Examples: PJ Ace's Kalshi spot, IM8, the "what is happening" comedic AI ad genre. **Why it works:** Leans into AI's weirdness instead of hiding it. Brand says "we're in on the joke." **Russell's edge:** This is THE growth vertical for AI-native commercial work in 2026 — fast turnaround, low budget, viral-by-design.

## Pipeline Note for SEALCaM

For each reference above, the recommended order of operations is:
1. **Subject** prompt: who/what is in frame, hero detail
2. **Environment** prompt: location, time of day, weather
3. **Action** prompt: motion verbs only (no adjectives)
4. **Lighting** prompt: key/fill/rim, color temp, source
5. **Camera** prompt: lens (mm), height, angle, movement
6. **Metatokens** prompt: medium (16mm / digital / archival), grain, framerate, aspect

Then run image-gen for hero frames → feed into video-gen with the same SEALCaM token block in the motion prompt to maintain consistency. References 1, 3, 8, 10, and 15 are the cleanest single-pass test cases to validate the pipeline before tackling the multi-character ones (4, 6, 9, 17).
